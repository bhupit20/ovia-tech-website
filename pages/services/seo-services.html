SEO strategy by intent

We build around the searches that can realistically become leads.

For service businesses, that usually means pages targeting buyer-ready searches like web development company, ecommerce development services, local SEO consultant, SaaS development agency, or industry-specific service terms. For local companies, it means combining location signals with specific service intent instead of relying on a single generic homepage.

The goal is to create a search path that feels natural: a visitor lands on a relevant page, understands the service quickly, sees trust signals, gets answers to common objections, and has a clear way to request a consultation.
