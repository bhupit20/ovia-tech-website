Website development FAQ

Questions businesses ask before starting a website project.

How much does a business website cost?

Website cost depends on the number of pages, content needs, integrations, design depth, CMS requirements, and timeline. We begin with a free consultation and recommend the most practical scope for your goals and budget.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can redesign your current site, improve structure, rewrite key messaging, optimize conversion paths, and rebuild the front end for better speed and mobile usability.

Will the website be SEO-ready?

Yes. We include SEO-aware page structure, metadata, semantic headings, internal linking recommendations, responsive performance basics, and clean technical foundations.

Do you write website content?

We can help shape headlines, service descriptions, CTA copy, and conversion-focused page content. If you already have content, we can refine it for clarity and persuasion.

Can you connect forms, analytics, or automation?

Yes. We can set up contact forms, quote forms, chatbot lead flows, analytics readiness, CRM handoff concepts, and automation paths depending on your workflow.

How long does a website project take?

Small focused websites can move quickly when content and scope are clear. Larger sites with custom sections, service pages, and integrations usually require a staged timeline.
