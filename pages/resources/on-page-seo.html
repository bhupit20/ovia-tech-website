FAQ

On-Page SEO questions.

Is on-page SEO different from technical SEO?

Yes. On-page SEO focuses on page content and structure, while technical SEO focuses on crawlability, speed, indexing, and site health.

Can you optimize existing pages?

Yes. We can improve current pages before creating new ones.

Do you write SEO copy?

Yes. We can rewrite page sections around search intent, clarity, and conversion.
