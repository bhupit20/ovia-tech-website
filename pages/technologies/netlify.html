What is Netlify?

Netlify gives teams a practical way to build static sites and serverless workflows.

Direct answer

Netlify is a jamstack hosting we use when a project needs simple deployments for content and frontend teams. It is a strong fit for Jamstack websites, landing pages, static sites, forms, serverless functions, and branch deploy previews.

Netlify helps teams build digital products with a clearer separation between content, design, infrastructure, and business logic. In practice, that means editors, developers, and founders can each work closer to their own strengths instead of fighting one rigid system.

Businesses use Netlify for projects where the website or app needs to stay fast, organized, and easier to improve over time. Common examples include service websites, SaaS marketing pages, ecommerce content, customer portals, campaign pages, and internal tools.

Why we use Netlify

Good technology should make the business easier to run after launch.

We use Netlify when it gives the client a simpler path to maintain, scale, and improve the website after launch. The right fit depends on the team, the content model, the SEO plan, the budget, and how often the product will change.

For Ovia Tech, the decision is practical: will it improve performance, publishing, SEO control, developer experience, or long-term maintainability? If the answer is no, we recommend a simpler path.

FAQ

Netlify development questions.

What is Netlify?

Netlify is a jamstack hosting used to build or support websites, applications, content workflows, and digital business systems. The exact role depends on the project architecture.

Why use Netlify?

Netlify is useful when it matches your team's workflow, publishing needs, performance goals, and maintenance plan. We recommend it only when the fit is practical.

Is Netlify good for SEO?

Yes, Netlify can support strong SEO when the frontend is built correctly, metadata is structured, pages load quickly, and content is organized around search intent.

How much does Netlify development cost?

Cost depends on page count, integrations, custom design, migration needs, content modeling, and QA. A small build is very different from an enterprise platform.

Is Netlify suitable for enterprise businesses?

Netlify can work for enterprise use cases when architecture, permissions, deployment, security, and content governance are planned properly.

Can Ovia Tech migrate an existing website to Netlify?

Yes. We can review your current website, content structure, URLs, SEO risks, and integrations before recommending a migration path.
