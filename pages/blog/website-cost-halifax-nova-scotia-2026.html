How Much Does a Website Cost in Halifax, Nova Scotia? (2026 Pricing Guide)

Website quotes in Halifax range from free to well over $20,000, and the reason why rarely gets explained. Here is what actually drives the price, broken down by the type of provider you hire.

Why Halifax Website Pricing Varies So Much

Ask five different people in Halifax what a website costs and you will get five very different numbers. That is not because anyone is being dishonest, it is because "a website" can mean a $12/month template with your logo dropped in, or a fully custom platform with booking systems, integrations, and a content team behind it. The price follows the scope, not the other way around.

Before you request quotes, it helps to understand the four common tiers Halifax and Nova Scotia businesses typically choose between, and what you actually get at each one.

DIY Website Builders: $0 to $50/month

Platforms like Wix, Squarespace, and GoDaddy Website Builder let you assemble a site yourself using templates. For a very early-stage business with no budget, this can be a reasonable starting point. The tradeoffs are real, though: generic designs, limited SEO control, and a site that will likely need a full rebuild once the business grows past its first year or two. We regularly get calls from Halifax business owners who outgrew a DIY site within 12 to 18 months.

Freelancer Pricing in Halifax: $800 to $3,000

Local and remote freelancers typically charge in this range for a small business website, often built on WordPress, Webflow, or Shopify. Quality varies enormously at this tier. Some freelancers are excellent and simply keep their overhead low. Others are juggling too many clients to give your project real attention. Ask about timeline, revisions, and what happens if they become unavailable mid-project, since a single freelancer with no backup plan is a real risk for a business that depends on its website.

Small Local Agency Pricing: $2,500 to $8,000

Small Halifax-area agencies and two-to-five-person studios typically fall in this range for a professional, multi-page business website. You get a team instead of one person, some SEO foundation, and generally more reliable project management than a solo freelancer. This tier is a common sweet spot for established local service businesses that need to look credible but do not require custom software.

Full-Service Agency Pricing: $5,000 to $25,000+

At the top end, full-service agencies (including Ovia Tech) build websites with custom design systems, deeper SEO structure, lead-generation systems, integrations with your CRM or booking software, and ongoing support. This tier makes sense for businesses that depend heavily on their website for revenue, are scaling into new markets, or need functionality beyond a standard brochure site. Custom web applications, booking platforms, and multi-location SEO structures push costs toward the higher end of this range.

What Actually Drives the Price Up

Regardless of which tier you choose, a few factors consistently move the price: the number of unique page templates (not just pages, since ten pages built from two templates costs less than ten pages built from ten templates), custom functionality like booking systems or client portals, content creation (copywriting and photography are frequently underestimated), and the depth of SEO work included. A five-page site with strong SEO structure often costs more than a fifteen-page site with none, because the SEO work is where the real time goes.

Hidden Costs Halifax Businesses Forget to Budget For

Domain registration and hosting (typically $150 to $600 per year, often overlooked when comparing a one-time design quote), ongoing maintenance and security updates, stock photography or a proper photo shoot, and copywriting if you do not plan to write the content yourself. Ask directly whether a quote includes content, hosting setup, and a launch-week support window, since these are the line items that most often turn a "final price" into a bigger invoice later.

How to Get an Accurate Quote

Come to the conversation with a clear list of pages you need, any specific functionality (booking, e-commerce, portals), and whether you have existing content and photography or need it created. A provider who gives you an exact number before understanding any of this is guessing, not quoting. A good process starts with a short discovery conversation and ends with a scoped, itemized proposal you can actually compare against other quotes.
